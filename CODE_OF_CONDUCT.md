# Code of Conduct

Bap ka Rasta welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience. This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, including with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Discuss code, designs, and decisions using evidence and specific examples.
- Give useful feedback, accept corrections, and acknowledge other people's work.
- Respect privacy, authorship, licenses, and personal boundaries.
- Keep discussions relevant and give others room to contribute.

Disagreement is welcome when it remains about the work. The game's street humour does not justify insulting or targeting contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing someone's private information without consent are not acceptable. Neither are spam, repeated disruptive behavior, or retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Reporting a concern

For a private project conduct report, email [tirthankar.dgen@gmail.com](mailto:tirthankar.dgen@gmail.com) with the subject **Bap ka Rasta conduct report**. Include links or other relevant context and describe the impact. Share only the personal information necessary to explain the concern.

For abuse that requires platform intervention, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). If a concern involves the person receiving project reports, that platform route remains available.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

Project maintainers may request changes, remove inappropriate content, lock a discussion, restrict participation, or block access to project spaces where platform permissions allow it. The response should reflect the severity, impact, and repetition of the behavior. Serious threats or exposure of private information may require immediate action.

Reports should be handled with discretion and shared only with people who need the information to address them. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised when platform intervention or legal obligations apply. A participant may request reconsideration through the same private contact and provide additional context.

This policy governs participation in the project. It is not a statement about employment, ownership, or licensing.
